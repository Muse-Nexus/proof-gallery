# Safety constitution

The purpose is access to concrete evidence of love, care, belonging, being
chosen or valued, capability, and accomplishment. It is not a productivity
ledger. An image may be the evidence without a written explanation; the system
must not manufacture its story, identities, relationships, or emotional meaning.

Never use Proof to invalidate pain, create guilt, demand optimism, or argue that
the user should feel better. Use it only to restore evidence that depression has
hidden.

- Never say, “But look how lucky you are.”
- Do not present evidence as proof that suffering is not real.
- Do not rank the user's worth.
- Do not fabricate emotional meaning.
- Preserve exact quotes, dates, and provenance.
- Show `MISSING` or omit an optional field when evidence is unavailable.
- Retrieval must be initiated by the user inside Proof Gallery.
- Do not automatically surface Proof during distress.
- Avoid sentimental, diagnostic, coercive, and falsely optimistic language.
- Return the saved evidence itself; do not manufacture an emotional conclusion.
- Short-note organization is a visible, optional word-rule suggestion, not an
  assessment. Respect manual changes and removals; negated or ambiguous cues
  must not be represented as a confident category. Crying alone does not imply
  recovery or explain an emotion. Never derive dates or people from a note.
- Related moments are user-requested matches within saved Proof only. Explain
  the shared words without asserting that the moments have a deeper connection.
- A story is currently an unsaved reading sequence of explicitly chosen saved
  notes and attachments, with exact words, dates, and sources. Keep unknown dates
  outside the chronology. Do not invent dialogue, causality, identities, lessons,
  emotional meaning, or optimistic endings. UI explanations are not quotes.

Features or contributions that weaken these rules are out of scope, even when
they appear engaging or helpful.
